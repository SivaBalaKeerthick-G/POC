About CogniDoc

Bridging the gap between vast organizational documentation and instant, verified AI answers.

Semantic Document Chunking

Complex enterprise files are parsed, structured, and indexed into contextual vector embeddings for high-precision retrieval.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Queries execute real-time semantic search to pull only the most relevant document chunks before formulating an answer.

Grounded & Verifiable Answers

Every response includes exact source citations, document filenames, and confidence match scores to eliminate hallucinations.

Built for High-Security Enterprise Workflows

CogniDoc ensures your internal IP remains protected while empowering teams to access information instantly.

Strict Access Control & Role Scoping Integrates with enterprise SSO and role-based policies (RBAC) to ensure users only retrieve documents they are authorized to see.
Zero Data Leakage Guarantee Your organizational documents and search queries are never used to train external public foundation models.
Instant Knowledge Updates As new policies or technical docs are uploaded, CogniDoc re-indexes chunks seamlessly for immediate query availability.
